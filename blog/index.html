Looking Ahead: My Plans After Graduation

I will graduate from William & Mary in May with a degree in Neuroscience and a minor in Data Science. As I approach the end of college, I have been thinking carefully about how I want to apply what I have learned to a professional career.

Over the past few years, I discovered that I am interested in the intersection of human behavior and data. My neuroscience coursework helped me understand how people think and make decisions, while my data science work gave me the tools to analyze patterns and turn information into insight.

After graduation, I plan to continue working in analytics, building experience analyzing operational data and supporting decision making in organizations. I am interested in roles that combine data analysis with communication and user-centered thinking, such as customer experience analytics, user experience research, or organizational decision support.

Long term, I hope to build a career that connects data with human behavior. Whether that involves improving user experiences, supporting better workplace decision making, or translating complex data into clear insights, I am excited to keep learning and developing these skills.

This website is part of that journey. It gives me a space to share my work, reflect on what I am learning, and document the projects and experiences that are shaping my career.
