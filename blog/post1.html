Looking Ahead: My Plans After Graduation

March 5th, 2026

I’ll graduate from William & Mary in May with a B.S. in Neuroscience and a minor in Data Science. Throughout my time in college, I became interested in how these two fields connect. Neuroscience helped me understand how people think, learn, and make decisions, while data science gave me the tools to analyze patterns and translate information into insights.

As I move into the next stage of my career, I hope to continue building experience in data analytics and decision support. My goal is to apply analytical thinking to real-world problems while keeping the human side of systems in mind. Data can tell us what is happening, but understanding behavior helps explain why.

Over time, I’m especially interested in opportunities that combine both perspectives. Areas like change management, customer experience, and user experience stand out to me because they focus on improving how people interact with systems, organizations, and technology.

This site is a place for me to document that process. It brings together my experiences and ideas that reflect how I’m continuing to develop at the intersection of neuroscience, data, and practical problem solving.
