Looking Ahead: My Plans After Graduation

March 2026

I’ll graduate from William & Mary in May with a B.S. in Neuroscience and a minor in Data Science. As I wrap up college, I’ve been thinking about how I want to combine what I’ve learned into a career path that feels practical and meaningful.

I’m especially interested in the intersection of human behavior and data: understanding how people make decisions, then using analytics and clear communication to improve outcomes.

After graduation, I plan to keep building experience in analytics and decision support. Longer term, I’m interested in roles that combine data with people-focused thinking like customer experience analytics, user experience research, or change management.

This site is part of that process — a place to document my experiences, share what I’m learning, and keep my work organized in one place.
